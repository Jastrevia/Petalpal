Contact us

For any question, problem or feedback about PetalPal, email contact@jastrevia.com. We are a small team and aim to reply within a few business days.

When you write to us, it helps to include the device and operating system you are using and a short description of what happened. Please do not include your password or the contents of private Journal entries.

Common questions

  • Delete my account: open the app and go to Settings → Delete Account. Deletion requires a recent sign-in and removes your data from our database. See Account & Data Deletion.
  • Trouble signing in: make sure you verified the email link we sent when you registered, then try again. If you still cannot get in, email us.
  • AI features: optional AI stays off until you agree, which we ask once when your Garden first opens. You can turn it off at any time in Settings.
  • Reporting someone: open a profile or a Flower message and choose Report or Block.

Important

PetalPal helps you record feelings and reflect. It is not a medical, counselling or emergency service and does not provide crisis monitoring. If you or someone else may be in danger, contact your local emergency number or a crisis line in your area.
