Who we are

PetalPal is operated by its co-founders, Xingran Ma and Jinyin Cao ("we", "us"), under the Jastrevia brand, in British Columbia, Canada. We are responsible for the personal information described in this policy. Contact us at contact@jastrevia.com.

PetalPal helps you record feelings and experiences, grow a visual Garden, share encouragement with friends and, if you choose, get AI-assisted reflections. It is not a medical, counselling or emergency service.

Who can use PetalPal

PetalPal is for adults only: you must be at least 19 years old, or the age of majority where you live, whichever is higher. PetalPal is not directed at children. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under this age, we will delete it.

Information we collect

  • Account: your email address, your sign-in identifier from Google Firebase, display name, chosen avatar, time zone and preferred language. Firebase handles your password and sign-in sessions; we never see your password.
  • What you create: private Journal entries and photos you attach, Events (text, date and the mood you choose), your Garden and Flowers, and the Weekly and Monthly reflections you generate.
  • Social: friend connections and requests, Garden visits, Flower messages, blocks and reports.
  • Optional AI data: suggested emotion labels, Event summaries and memory embeddings, reflections and the references that support them, and voice recordings you choose to send for transcription.
  • Technical records: consent and version records, security events, request identifiers, minimal records of AI usage cost, and deletion records. Our hosting providers may also process your IP address and basic request information to deliver the service.

How we use it

We use this information to run your account, save your entries, draw your Garden, deliver the social features you use, respect your privacy and AI choices, review reports, keep the service secure and complete deletion requests. We do not sell your personal information and we do not show advertising.

Who can see what

Journals and attached photos are visible only to you and are never used for AI memory or reflections. Events and everything AI derives from them are visible only to you. Friends can see your display name, avatar, Garden flowers, visits and the messages you send them, but never the text of your Events or Journals. Please don't put sensitive information about yourself or others into messages. Blocking someone stops contact and Garden access both ways; it does not remove copies they already received.

AI features are optional

AI is off by default. In Settings you choose separately whether to allow AI Processing (emotion suggestions and voice transcription), Personalization (personalized reflections from your Events) and Long-term Memory (saved Event summaries used in Weekly and Monthly reflections). Settings shows which service handles each feature.

When a feature is on, we send only what it needs: Event text, the mood you chose, dates, derived memories and short Event excerpts, or a recording you submit for transcription. Journals and photos are never sent. Fairy Moments captions are written without any of your Events or Journals. You can turn these choices off at any time; this stops future processing but cannot recall data already sent. Turning off Long-term Memory deletes the saved memories and reflections in our database while keeping your original entries. AI can be wrong, and its output is not a diagnosis or professional advice.

Service providers

We use these providers to run PetalPal: Google Firebase (sign-in), Cloudflare (website delivery, Workers AI and AI Gateway), Render (our server and PostgreSQL database) and Anthropic (AI-written reflections and captions, reached through Cloudflare AI Gateway). They process information on our behalf to provide these services. Your information may be processed outside Canada, including in the United States, where it is subject to local law.

On your device

The app and website store your sign-in session, preferences and some Garden display data on your device or in your browser. Signing out and deleting your account clear the app's own stored data.

How long we keep it, and deletion

We keep your information while your account is active and until you delete it. You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete Account, or by emailing us (see Account & Data Deletion). When you delete your account, your account and its data are removed from our database right away. To protect against data loss, we make regular backup copies of our database and keep each copy for no more than 30 days. A deleted account can remain in backups made before the deletion until those backups are deleted, at most 30 days later. We use backups only to recover from a technical failure, never to restore a deleted account, and we do not keep backups of deleted accounts beyond those 30 days.

A few minimal records that contain none of your entries, such as security events, AI cost records and the record that a deletion happened, are kept only as long as needed to protect the service and confirm the deletion. Messages already delivered to other people and data already sent to an AI provider may remain with them under their own retention.

Your rights

You can ask to access or correct your personal information, withdraw consent, or delete your account by writing to contact@jastrevia.com from your account email. We will reply within 30 days. We handle personal information under Canadian privacy law, including PIPEDA and British Columbia's Personal Information Protection Act. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia.

Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), sign-in checks on every request and access controls so that only you can reach your private data. No system is perfectly secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here with a new effective date. If we change how AI processes your information in a significant way, we will ask for your choice again in the app.
