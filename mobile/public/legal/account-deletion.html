Delete in the app

Open Settings, choose Delete Account, read the explanation and confirm Permanently delete my account. If you signed in more than a few minutes ago, you will be asked to sign in again first. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Request deletion by email

You can also ask us to delete your account without opening the app. Email contact@jastrevia.com from the email address on your PetalPal account and ask us to delete your account and its data. Never send your password, verification codes or Journal text. We check that the request comes from the account's owner, then complete it and confirm by email within 30 days. If you no longer have access to that mailbox, write to us and we will explain how to verify your identity another way.

Open deletion request email

What deletion removes

Your profile, private Journals and attached photos, Events, AI memories and embeddings, saved reflections and their references, your Garden and Flowers, the messages you sent, friendships and requests, blocks, and reports made by or about your account are removed from our database right away. Your sign-in identity with Google Firebase is deleted too; if Firebase is briefly unavailable, we retry until it succeeds. Our database backups are each kept for no more than 30 days, so your data can remain in backups made before the deletion for up to 30 days, after which those backups are deleted. Backups are used only to recover from a technical failure and are never used to restore a deleted account; we do not keep backups of deleted accounts beyond those 30 days. The app's own stored data on your device is cleared when deletion finishes.

What may remain

A few minimal records that contain none of your entries, such as security events, AI cost records and the record that the deletion happened, are kept only as long as needed to protect the service and confirm the deletion. Messages other people already received stay with them. Data already sent to an AI provider follows that provider's own retention; contact us if you have questions about it.
