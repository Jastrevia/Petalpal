# Emotional safety boundaries

PetalPal is a journal-first reflection product. User-selected Primary Mood remains authoritative. Suggested secondary emotions are uncertain labels, not diagnoses; no clinician, therapy, treatment, prevention or guaranteed emotional-outcome claim is made.

Local UI notes on Event, private Journal and Garden message composition provide a standing immediate-danger help path. On top of it, `safetyConcern` (`mobile/src/services/emotionalSafety.ts`) reads the writing on the device in English and Chinese (Simplified and common Traditional forms) for three worries: suicide or self-harm, killing or hurting someone, and being hurt or in danger. When one matches, a caring window (`mobile/src/components/features/SafetySupport.tsx`) opens after a short pause in typing, or at the latest on Save, and a shorter note stays beside the writing. It says what to do now (a crisis line, emergency services or the police, a trusted person, the nearest hospital emergency department), and never uploads text, blocks saving or notifies anyone. It matches words, not meaning: a quote or a story can match, and writing phrased in other ways will not. This is not risk assessment, comprehensive detection or an emergency service.

Numbers are chosen from the device time zone, on the device: mainland China 12356 and 110, Hong Kong 2389 2222 and 999, Taiwan 1925 and 110, US and Canada 988 and 911, UK and Ireland 116 123 and 112, Australia 13 11 14 and 000, New Zealand 1737 and 111, the rest of Europe 112. Anywhere else it says to call the local emergency number and links findahelpline.com. Recheck these numbers at each store submission; a person travelling may see the numbers of the zone their device is set to.

Reflection UI repeats the non-medical boundary and offers private-output reporting. Existing grounded report validation, closed Sonnet/Opus contracts and emotion taxonomy remain. Workers report instructions now explicitly exclude treatment, clinical authority, guaranteed outcomes and emergency-monitoring claims. Prompts alone are not a safety guarantee: continue reviewing reports, synthetic regressions and output constraints. This change does not authorize paid provider evaluation.

Evidence: `mobile/test/emotionalSafety.test.mjs`, `test/back/content-safety.test.js`, existing grounded report/prompt-injection suites. Human clinical/legal review of product positioning and supported locales is outstanding. No licensed-clinician review is claimed.
