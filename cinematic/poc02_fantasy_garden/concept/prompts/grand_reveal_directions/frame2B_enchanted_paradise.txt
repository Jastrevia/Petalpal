Cinematic fantasy realism with refined painterly stylisation: a single frame from a high-budget fantasy feature film, shot on a large-format digital cinema camera with soft spherical prime lenses and graded by a feature-film colourist. The world is the Hidden Valley of PetalPal: a secluded high valley of pale ivory limestone softened by moss and ferns, luminous clear turquoise water (jewel-like #5AA4AB deepening to teal), long cascades of lavender-violet wisteria (#A67EB7), drifts of crocus flowers with saffron-gold stamens, sage and moss greens, warm honey-gold accents. Early-morning golden sunlight from the left, gentle volumetric haze and light shafts, cool airy shadows that never go black, soft highlight bloom, natural depth of field, fine film grain. Mood: gentle, welcoming, quietly magical; companionship and belonging, never epic, dark or menacing. Grade: soft contrast, slightly lifted shadows with a hint of teal, warm honey highlights, saturation restrained everywhere except water and blossom. Physically believable materials: wet stone, translucent petals, soft moss, clear refractive water.

THE GRAND REVEAL — ENCHANTED PARADISE. An intimate, low reveal of the hidden valley from the water's edge, about one metre above crystalline turquoise shallows, looking up the lake toward the valley head; 40mm lens at f/2.8, focus on blossom about four metres away, the far valley melting into soft bokeh. Botanical detail is the subject. Across the upper left, a hanging curtain of lavender-violet wisteria racemes pours from an unseen tree above, every floret distinct, petals translucent and glowing where sunlight passes through, the nearest strands soft and out of focus. Lower right, a mossy limestone terrace carries a dense, natural drift of crocus in lavender, violet and ivory, cups half open to the sun, saffron-gold stamens glowing, dew on the petals, delicate fern fronds and tiny white blossoms among them. Across the bottom third the shallows are glass-clear: rounded pale pebbles and moss-green stones beneath gently rippling water, caustic light patterns dancing over them, a few wisteria petals floating and casting tiny shadows on the bed. Beyond the blossom, softly defocused, the lake opens out: at the valley head right of centre a slender waterfall reads as a luminous veil of light and mist; a small island pavilion with a slate-blue tiled roof is a soft silhouette; cliffs dressed in cascading wisteria fade into golden haze. The low morning sun is upper left and slightly behind the blossom, so petals, fern edges and drifting pollen are backlit and rim-lit; soft light shafts slant through the hanging blossom; dreamy highlight bloom, glowing air. Lush, tender and paradisiacal, like stepping into a secret garden.

No people, no fairies or other characters, no animals in the foreground, no text, letters, signs, logos or watermarks. Avoid: oversaturated neon colours, glitter, sparkle overload, lens-flare streaks, plastic CGI surfaces, video-game or mobile-game rendering, flat digital illustration, cartoon proportions, castles or cities, symmetrical centred composition, mushy undefined foliage, duplicated or impossible waterfalls, warped architecture.
