Cinematic fantasy realism with refined painterly stylisation: a single frame from a high-budget fantasy feature film, shot on a large-format digital cinema camera with soft spherical prime lenses and graded by a feature-film colourist. The world is the Hidden Valley of PetalPal: a secluded high valley of pale ivory limestone softened by moss and ferns, luminous clear turquoise water (jewel-like #5AA4AB deepening to teal), long cascades of lavender-violet wisteria (#A67EB7), drifts of crocus flowers with saffron-gold stamens, sage and moss greens, warm honey-gold accents. Early-morning golden sunlight from the left, gentle volumetric haze and light shafts, cool airy shadows that never go black, soft highlight bloom, natural depth of field, fine film grain. Mood: gentle, welcoming, quietly magical; companionship and belonging, never epic, dark or menacing. Grade: soft contrast, slightly lifted shadows with a hint of teal, warm honey highlights, saturation restrained everywhere except water and blossom. Physically believable materials: wet stone, translucent petals, soft moss, clear refractive water.

THE GRAND REVEAL — PORTAL REVEAL. The reveal seen from inside the passage that leads into the valley: first-person view at standing eye height, about 1.6 metres, a few steps back from the passage mouth; 24mm lens, camera level, deep focus from the arch to the valley. The nearer part of the frame is a shadowy tunnel of living blossom: pale limestone walls and a low vault almost completely clothed in wisteria vines, ferns and moss, long lavender racemes hanging from the vault along the top edge of the frame, their tips glowing where stray light catches them. The tunnel lies in deep cool violet-teal shade, about three stops darker than outside, yet every surface still holds detail; nothing goes black. A path of worn, damp flagstones with moss in the joints and a few crocus between them runs from the bottom of the frame straight through the opening and out onto a sunlit stone landing beyond, clear and unobstructed, as if inviting the next step; the lower middle of the frame is open. The opening is a tall, slightly irregular natural arch of limestone with a delicate antique gilded-bronze arch of fine leaf filigree set within it, wound with wisteria; its centre sits a little left of the frame centre and it spans about half the frame width. Through it the valley blazes in golden morning light: a luminous turquoise lake below, a tall elegant two-tier waterfall on the right, a small island pavilion with a slate-blue tiered roof and upswept eaves, cliffs with hanging gardens, layered mountains in golden haze. Warm light floods through the arch in volumetric beams across the flagstones, rim-lighting the filigree, the hanging blossom and the moss, and catching drifting petals and motes. Outside is bright but not blown out. The passage from cool shade to warm valley light is the emotional heart of the frame: safety behind, wonder ahead.

No people, no fairies or other characters, no animals in the foreground, no text, letters, signs, logos or watermarks.
