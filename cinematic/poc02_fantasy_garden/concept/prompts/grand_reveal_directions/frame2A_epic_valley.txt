Cinematic fantasy realism with refined painterly stylisation: a single frame from a high-budget fantasy feature film, shot on a large-format digital cinema camera with soft spherical prime lenses and graded by a feature-film colourist. The world is the Hidden Valley of PetalPal: a secluded high valley of pale ivory limestone softened by moss and ferns, luminous clear turquoise water (jewel-like #5AA4AB deepening to teal), long cascades of lavender-violet wisteria (#A67EB7), drifts of crocus flowers with saffron-gold stamens, sage and moss greens, warm honey-gold accents. Early-morning golden sunlight from the left, gentle volumetric haze and light shafts, cool airy shadows that never go black, soft highlight bloom, natural depth of field, fine film grain. Mood: gentle, welcoming, quietly magical; companionship and belonging, never epic, dark or menacing. Grade: soft contrast, slightly lifted shadows with a hint of teal, warm honey highlights, saturation restrained everywhere except water and blossom. Physically believable materials: wet stone, translucent petals, soft moss, clear refractive water.

THE GRAND REVEAL — EPIC VALLEY. A vast hidden valley seen from high on its southern rim, about 150 metres above an immense turquoise lake, from a bare limestone promontory at the very edge; 21mm lens, camera level with the horizon on the upper third, deep focus to infinity. The lake stretches away for more than a kilometre and fills the lower half of the 16:9 frame: crystal-clear shallows over pale stone near the shore, deepening through jewel turquoise to saturated teal, mirroring the cliffs. Colossal pale ivory limestone walls several hundred metres high enclose the valley, stepped with ledges of hanging gardens, ferns and cascading wisteria. Right of centre, at the far head of the valley, one majestic waterfall drops in three tiers down the full height of the cliffs from a notch in the rim into a wide, misty plunge pool; its sunlit veil of spray is the brightest point in the frame. On the left-hand cliffs, two far slenderer ribbon falls, each from its own separate cleft, thread down into the lake in the middle distance, fainter so the hero fall dominates. Far below on the left, a colossal ancient tree on a cliff shelf, its canopy hazy with lavender wisteria. In the middle of the lake, a small island with a delicate pavilion under a slate-blue tiered roof, so small it reads only as a jewel-like accent that reveals the true scale. Terraced shores carry faint lavender and ivory drifts of crocus. Beyond the rim, range after range of misty forested mountains recede into warm golden haze, with a soft bank of morning cloud lying in the far valley below the peaks. Low golden sun from the left rakes across the walls, carving long volumetric god rays through mist and spray; mist drifts over the far water. In the bottom-left corner, the mossy edge of the promontory with a few crocus and one trailing wisteria strand, softly out of focus, anchors the viewer. Monumental and awe-inspiring in scale, yet calm, luminous and welcoming in mood; never dark or ominous.

No people, no fairies or other characters, no animals in the foreground, no text, letters, signs, logos or watermarks.
