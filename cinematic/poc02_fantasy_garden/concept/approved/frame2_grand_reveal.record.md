# Visual record: frame2_grand_reveal.png (approved anchor)

Frame 2 · The Grand Reveal · Direction C, Portal Reveal. Approved 2026-10-20. Measurements are on the 1672 × 941
image (x from the left, y from the top). Where the image differs from the written Direction C spec, **the image wins**.

## Composition and camera (estimated)
- First-person view, standing eye height (about 1.6 m), camera level, roughly a 24 mm full-frame lens (about 74° across).
- Frame within a frame: the stone mouth of the passage spans about x 540–1276 and from above the top of frame down to
  the landing at y ≈ 712; its centre sits slightly right of and above the frame centre (about 52% across, 46% down).
- The camera is on the path centreline, roughly 5–7 m short of the gilded arch.
- Layering: passage walls and hanging wisteria → gilded arch → stone landing and parapet → turquoise lake and pavilion
  island → karst cliff towers and waterfall → layered mountains and sky.

## Environment landmarks
1. **Passage mouth.** An irregular natural vault of pale ivory limestone, its edge heavy with wisteria; thick, twisting
   woody wisteria trunks climb both walls. The left wall is darker and more overgrown; the right wall has large pale
   boulders.
2. **Gilded arch** (inset in the mouth), deliberately asymmetric:
   - left side: a double-railed gilded-bronze curve with leaf filigree, gilded leaves and curling tendrils, rising
     from a post at x ≈ 520–545 and arching over to about the frame centre at the top;
   - right side: a straight upright post of braided, vine-like gilded filigree at x ≈ 1280–1340;
   - hanging from the top curve: three small gilded lanterns and one bare drop, each ending in a turquoise teardrop
     crystal (at about x 587, 700, 785 and 825);
   - wisteria trained through the whole arch.
3. **Path.** Large, irregular, wet flagstones with moss in the joints and scattered fallen lavender petals; sun
   reflections glint along them. The path runs from the bottom of the frame to a stone landing at the mouth.
4. **Landing.** Weathered squared ivory limestone blocks form a broken low parapet on the left beyond the arch, with
   a single block post on the right. The lake lies below the landing.
5. **Foreground planting.** Lavender-violet and white crocus with saffron-orange stigmas, the largest at bottom right;
   ferns, moss and small lavender flowers along the bottom left.
6. **Pavilion** (centre, x ≈ 735–895, y ≈ 438–588; about 9% of frame width):
   - two-tier roof of slate-blue tiles edged in gold, with upturned gilded eave tips and a tall gold spire finial;
   - pale cream columns, a balustrade and front steps facing the camera;
   - wisteria cascading from the left eaves;
   - it stands on a mossy rock island whose edges spill small cascades into the lake;
   - a single-arched stone bridge links the island to the left shore (x ≈ 600–690).
7. **Waterfall** (right, x ≈ 1100–1240, y ≈ 240–615): one tall fall in four stages (an upper fall from a cleft, a
   long main drop, a broad lower cascade over boulders, small falls at the base) with wide sunlit mist into the lake.
8. **Cliffs.** Towering karst-like limestone pillars crowned with trees and draped in wisteria, separated by layers of
   mist.
9. **Distance.** Layered golden mountains (y ≈ 210–300) and a pale gold sky with soft clouds.
10. **Water.** Luminous turquoise lake, clear near the island with visible stones and ripples.

## Light and atmosphere
- Backlit golden hour. The sun is low and almost straight ahead, slightly left, just behind the upper-left curve of the
  gilded arch; the brightest sky is around (750, 200).
- God rays slant down from the upper left through the valley mist.
- Warm light spills through the mouth onto the wet flagstones as a band of glints toward the camera.
- The passage interior is **warm, dim amber-brown** (wall median ≈ #3A2B1A to #382B1D), not cool teal. The blossom
  glows where light reaches it. The valley is about 2–2.5 stops brighter than the passage walls.
- Mist and haze: in the valley, around the waterfall and at the cliff bases.
